# Changelog

## 0.2.1

More compact settings and a manageable saved quote library.

- Settings tabs have more padding and respond across their entire button area.
- Removed the extra General explanations and the redundant display section heading.
- Calendar accounts appear beside calendar names in compact, single-line rows.
- Search saved quotes by text, author or source, and browse five quotes per page.
- Show any saved quote directly from its row.
- Updated Turkish translations.

## 0.2.0

A refreshed Mac interface inspired by Brimlet’s graphite and mint icon.

- Five native settings tabs: General, Modules, Calendar, Notifications and Quotes, in a compact 520 pt window.
- Settings, task entry and quote cards follow the Mac’s light or dark appearance.
- Graphite surfaces, clearer text and more focused mint accents in the notch panel.
- Updated music controls, calendar rows, task filters and quote cards.
- Drag to reorder modules, with the existing up/down buttons also available.
- Calendar colors and app names make calendars and allowed notification senders easier to recognize.
- Updated English and Turkish interface text and keyboard controls.

## 0.1.0

First public release.

- A panel around the MacBook notch, or a small capsule at the top of displays without one, that opens on hover or click and shows what is going on while it is small.
- Music from the Spotify app: track, artist, album art, seek bar, playback controls, a peek with controls when the track changes, and lyrics from LRCLIB.
- Timer with 5, 15, 25 and 45 minutes, pause and an optional sound.
- Calendar, off by default: today, tomorrow or seven days, Join buttons for Meet, Zoom, Teams and Webex, and reminders before meetings.
- Tasks with categories, due dates and reminders.
- Quotes saved from the Services menu, shown now and then on a card of their own.
- Notifications from other apps the user allows, over a local socket that checks both apps' signatures.
- Update check: once a day Brimlet asks GitHub for the latest version number, and Download and Install verifies the disk image before opening it. Releases are published as `Brimlet.dmg` with its checksum in `Brimlet.dmg.sha256`.
- English and Turkish.
